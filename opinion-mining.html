About the Project

Opinion mining (sentiment analysis) extracts what customers feel from the text they write.

💬 The problem

Thousands of reviews are impossible to read by hand. Businesses need an automatic way to see whether customers are happy.

🎯 The goal

Build an application that labels each review as positive, negative or neutral and summarizes the overall opinion.

🧠 The approach

Text is cleaned and tokenized, then classified by a Naive Bayes model and a lexicon-based scorer, both implemented from scratch.

🌍 Applications

Product feedback analysis, brand monitoring, customer support triage, market research and recommendation systems.

The Dataset

A hand-written demo corpus of product reviews, balanced across three classes. It is small on purpose so every result is easy to inspect; larger corpora such as Amazon reviews are listed under Future Scope.
